# Thistledown & Pine Returns and Shipping Policy

Thistledown & Pine is a fictional online retailer of handmade home goods,
used here only to test chunking, embedding, and retrieval. This policy
contains no real company, product, or customer information.

## Refund Policy

### Standard Refund Window

Most items purchased from Thistledown & Pine may be returned within 30
days of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment
method. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include
all tags. Refunds are issued within 5 to 7 business days after we receive
and inspect the returned item.

### Final Sale and Non-Returnable Items

Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout, gift cards, and custom or
made-to-order pieces (for example, engraved cutting boards or
custom-dyed textiles) cannot be returned or refunded under any
circumstances, except where required by law.

### Damaged or Defective Items

If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 14 days
of delivery with photos of the damage. We will issue a full refund,
including original shipping cost, or send a free replacement, whichever
the customer prefers. Damaged-item claims made after 14 days are handled
at the discretion of the support team and are not guaranteed a refund.

### Already-Refunded Orders

An order can only be refunded once. If a refund has already been issued
for an order, a second refund request for the same order will be denied
and the customer will be referred to a human support agent.

### Refunds Above $250

Any single refund request over $250 requires manager approval before it
is issued. Approved refunds over this threshold are still processed
within the standard 5 to 7 business day window once approved; the
approval step itself typically takes 1 additional business day.

## Shipping Policy

### Domestic Shipping (United States)

Standard domestic shipping takes 4 to 6 business days and costs $6.99,
or is free on orders over $75. Expedited shipping (2 to 3 business days)
is available for $14.99 at checkout.

### International Shipping

Thistledown & Pine ships to Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia.
International orders take 10 to 18 business days and cost a flat $24.99.
Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged
by their country; these are never included in the order total.

### Late or Missing Deliveries

If a tracked order shows no movement for more than 7 calendar days past
its estimated delivery date, contact support with the order number for
an investigation. Confirmed lost packages are either reshipped at no
cost or fully refunded, customer's choice. Orders that show as
"delivered" by the carrier but were not received must be reported within
7 days of the delivery scan for a replacement or refund to be considered.

### Address Changes and Order Cancellation

An order's shipping address can be changed only before it has shipped.
Once an order status changes to "Shipped," the address can no longer be
modified, and Thistledown & Pine is not responsible for packages sent to
an address the customer entered incorrectly. Orders can be cancelled for
a full refund any time before they ship; once shipped, the order follows
the standard refund policy above instead.

## Contacting Support

Support is available by email only, Monday through Friday, 9am to 5pm
Eastern Time. Response times are typically within 1 business day. Order
number and the account email used at checkout should be included in every
request so the order can be located quickly.
